Real-Time Deepfake Detection & Diagnostic Reporting System

Analyze a video playing on this laptop and download a diagnostic report.

How to use

Put the video on one side of your screen and this page in a small, separate window on the other side. Keep this page outside the area you select, so clicking Stop does not cover your video.

  1. Click Start and switch to your video during the countdown.
  2. In the selection window, drag a box around the face and press Enter.
  3. Play the video. Keep its window in the same place.
  4. Return here and click Stop. Wait for the verdict and report.
